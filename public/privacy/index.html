SafeMask Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Domain: https://safemask.tuandigital.vn

1. Local On-Device Processing

SafeMask processes your photos, PDFs, EXIF/GPS metadata, and text locally on your device. We NEVER upload your images, documents, IDs, SSNs, phone numbers, emails, bank accounts, or GPS coordinates to any server.

2. Ephemeral Session Memory

AI Privacy Copy stores token-to-original mappings strictly in temporary RAM and wipes them when you tap Reset or leave the screen.

3. Clear Network Separation

Core redaction, PDF rasterization, EXIF cleaning, and text sanitization work offline. On-device OCR downloads its local language model once on first scan and caches it on your device. Pro purchases connect only to Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing.

4. Contact

Privacy questions: SafeMask Support.
