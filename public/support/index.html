Need help with SafeMask on iOS, Android APK, or Web PWA?

Quick Help

  • iOS Save Image: Tap Save or Share Safe and choose "Save Image" in the iOS share sheet.
  • Restore Purchases: Open Settings → SafeMask Pro → Restore Purchases.
  • Android APK Install: Download safemask-latest.apk and allow installation from your browser.
